Eurovision Lyrics: What Are We Really Singing About?

A lyrical theme analysis of every Eurovision entry from 1956 to 2025 — 1,795 songs, 10 themes, and some surprising patterns about what actually wins.

1,795 songs analysed 70 years of Eurovision 10 lyrical themes 71 winners
The 10 Themes of Eurovision
What is Eurovision actually singing about? Party & celebration dominates — nearly half of all entries — but that was not always the case.
46% of all Eurovision songs are about partying or celebrating. Yet love (21%) still produces more winners per entry.
Which Theme Actually Wins?
Win rate = % of songs in each theme that took home the trophy. The most common theme is not the best at winning.
War songs: 40 entries, 0 wins — ever. And love songs outperform party songs in win rate (5.8% vs 4.3%).
How Eurovision Themes Changed Across 7 Decades
Theme proportion per decade reveals a dramatic cultural shift. The 2000s broke everything: party music went from 25% to nearly 80% by the 2010s.
The great party takeover: In the 1960s, 41% of songs were love songs. By the 2010s, party/celebration was 77% of all entries. Love fell from 41% to 10%.
12 Points: Jury vs Public Taste by Theme
The coveted douze points per theme, split by jury vs televote. Some themes are loved by the crowd but ignored by experts.
The public gives 12s to hope and nostalgia — the jury never does. War songs collect 4 jury 12-points despite never winning the contest.
